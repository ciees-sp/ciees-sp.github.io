If an objective notes an application gap, CIEES is requesting additional SME input to identify observable practitioner behavior that would be seen on the job if they demonstrated this objective correctly so it can be practiced and assessed within the module. Please provide one or two application insights showing how practitioners apply this objective, for example:

  • what practitioners need to do or decide when applying the content;
  • what they may need to adjust based on the individual or situation;
  • a boundary or role clarification they need to recognize; or
  • something that is easily misunderstood or confused by new practitioners.

This might include examples of common scenarios or situations they should be prepared to respond to in their daily work. A brief overview is helpful, CIEES will develop the full scenarios and practice activities.
