Headings

Headings appear in NVDA as a tree, indented by level.
Current: every category, division, school, and section is an H2; all items sit at one level with no nesting.
Required: category = H2; division = H3. A division-wide division places its sections at H4. A school-based division places each school at H4 and that school's sections at H5.
Table of Contents entries are links and do not appear in this list.
Reference: WCAG 1.3.1.

Reference: WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices, landmark regions.
